# Multi-field campaign experiments: protocol

Protocol of the experiments reported in "Multi-Field Coverage Path Planning for a Truck-Carried Heterogeneous UAV Fleet: Joint Genetic Optimization Versus a Two-Stage Planner" (submitted to IEEE Access, 2026). The commands are listed in the repository README; this file records what the job matrix contains, so that the shipped result files can be checked against it.

## Campaigns

Generated by `scripts/exp_campaigns.py` (eleven base campaigns and three road-side variants), `scripts/exp_campaign_varied.py` (C5varied) and `scripts/exp_campaign_big.py` (C3big). Field shapes come from five templates (irregular quadrilateral, trapezoid, clipped-corner pentagon, triangular wedge, non-convex L), scaled to the target area and rotated per field; only the two C2 controls keep square fields. Default parcel 100 ha, service road along the southern side, grid step 200 m, truck speed 40 km/h, launch price 3 $/launch, crew rate 10 $/h.

| Name in files | Paper name | N | Geometry | Probes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C2close | C2-close | 2 | Adjacent square fields, ~1.4 km apart | Transit negligible |
| C2far | C2-far | 2 | Square fields ~46 km apart | Transit dominates |
| C3line | C3-line | 3 | Collinear parcels, gaps 2.8 and 39 km | Default order already optimal |
| C3tri | C3-tri | 3 | Identical rotated pentagons on an equilateral triangle | Ordering irrelevant by symmetry |
| C3big | C3-big | 3 | ~780, 400 and 100 ha parcels | Within-field parallelism, drone sequencing |
| C5mixed | C5-mixed | 5 | Two 2-field clusters + one far field | Cluster grouping |
| C5holes | C5-obstacles | 5 | 100–203 ha parcels, three with 1–3 obstacles | Obstacles + ordering |
| C5varied | C5-varied | 5 | Elongated fields at five orientations | Per-field flight direction |
| C5size | C5-size | 5 | Areas spanning 16× (256–16 ha) | Per-field drone selection |
| C10grid | C10-grid | 10 | 2 × 5 grid of rotated mixed-shape parcels | Scaling, real tour space |
| C15scatter | C15-scatter | 15 | Scattered, all shapes, ~20 × 21 km | Full-day stress test |
| C5mixedR, C5sizeR, C10gridR | C5-mixed-R, C5-size-R, C10-grid-R | 5, 5, 10 | Same parcels, road on the north side of every second field | Road-side control study |

`scripts/exp_manifest.json` maps these names to campaign ids in the local database; the shipped result files refer to ids 53–66.

## Fleet

Five drone classes, created by `exp_campaigns.get_fleet()` (`FLEET_SPECS`). From the light to the heavy class the range and the cruise speed increase, the maneuverability decreases, the per-launch cost increases and the per-flight-hour cost decreases.

| Class | Cruise, km/h | Range, km | Turn slowdown, %/° | Speed floor, % | $/launch | $/km | $/h |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scout (Mini-class) | 58 | 12 | 0.20 | 40 | 0.30 | 0.010 | 4.80 |
| Light (Phantom-class) | 72 | 14 | 0.40 | 15 | 0.80 | 0.020 | 3.00 |
| Mid (Mavic-class) | 72 | 37 | 0.50 | 10 | 1.70 | 0.045 | 2.40 |
| Long (Evo-class) | 72 | 48 | 0.65 | 5 | 3.00 | 0.060 | 1.60 |
| Heavy (M300-class) | 82 | 60 | 0.80 | 3 | 6.00 | 0.080 | 1.00 |

Shared settings: working-speed cap 7 km/h while spraying, soft day limit 8 h (linear overtime penalty), hard limit 14 h (exponential penalty), batteries swapped on the truck between flights.

## Optimizer settings

Population 50, 40 generations, 2000 evaluations per run, mutation chance 0.1 per gene, tournament selection, (μ+λ) elitism, 8 seeds per configuration. The random stream is keyed by the full configuration identity, so no two configurations share random numbers. The canonical configuration is the permutation encoding of the order gene with OX crossover and inversion mutation. Random search draws the same number of plans from the same generator and records its best-so-far every 50 samples. Comparisons use the two-sided Mann–Whitney U test over the 8 seeds; the smallest achievable p for 8 vs 8 is 1.6 × 10⁻⁴ (complete separation).

## Job matrix

### Main matrix, `scripts/exp_results.jsonl`, 640 runs

`exp_run.py --ngen 40 --pop 50 --seeds 8 --nn_seed --transit_roles C5mixed,C10grid`

| Block | Campaigns | Configurations | Runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canonical joint GA | all 11 | full, OX + inversion | 88 |
| Random search, matched budget | all 11 | | 88 |
| Ablations: one dimension frozen at its default | C3line, C5mixed, C5holes, C10grid, C5varied, C5size, C3big, C15scatter | fixed_order (storage order), fixed_direction (east–west lines), fixed_start (SW corner), fixed_drones (one mid-class drone) | 256 |
| Granularity: dimension optimized as one campaign-wide value | C5size, C5holes, C10grid | single_direction, single_start, single_drones | 72 |
| Two-stage planner, nearest-neighbour tour (`ga_nn`) | C5mixed, C5holes, C10grid, C5varied, C5size, C15scatter | order frozen, full budget on coverage genes | 48 |
| Truck-speed sweep | C5mixed, C10grid | 20, 60, 80 km/h (40 is canonical) | 48 |
| Random-keys encoding of the order gene | C10grid, C15scatter | rk + rk | 16 |
| Operator grid, robustness check | C10grid | 8 remaining OX/PMX/CX × swap/insert/inversion pairs, 3 seeds each | 24 |

### Ordering subproblem (no field coverage involved)

- `scripts/exp_ordering.py` → `scripts/exp_ordering.json`: directed transit matrix per campaign; exact optimal and worst open tours (Held–Karp), nearest-neighbour tour (best start field), default storage order, with gaps over the optimum.
- `scripts/exp_tsp_operators.py` → `scripts/exp_tsp.json`: permutation GA on the tour problem alone, 9 crossover × mutation pairs plus the random-keys encoding, 30 repetitions each, population 50, 60 generations; mean gap over the exact optimum.

## Outputs

`scripts/exp_analyze.py` writes `experiments/results_tables.md` (Tables 1–9), `experiments/results_summary.json` (the same aggregates as JSON) and the statistical figures; `scripts/exp_figures_maps.py` draws the campaign gallery and the solved example plans. The README maps every table and figure of the paper to these outputs.

Coverage is verified in every run from the unique grid points visited (`final.grid_total`, `final.grid_missed`); all 784 runs cover 100% of their grids.

## Compute

Workstation with an Intel Core i7-1260P, 14 worker processes. One run takes 1.5 min (N = 2) to 14 min (C3-big) in a single process. Main matrix 75 CPU-hours (about 5.5 h wall-clock), control study 10 CPU-hours, exact-tour comparison 3 CPU-hours.
